# AgentAvatar

An agent's portrait in a soft glass orb with its name tag below: the main way Ivy, Cooper, Lana and Elliot appear.

- The consumer provides `src` (the agent's portrait asset), `name`, `agent` (tints the orb's glow with that agent's mid gradient stop) and optional `size` (default 160) and `live`.
- Put it on the agent's own gradient (`gradient-<agent>`) or on `surface-150`. Pair it with that agent's mark for orbit lines.
- Show the four agents in the fixed order Ivy, Cooper, Elliot, Lana, as in the guide.
- Do not crop the portrait into a square, and do not add a border ring, badge or colored overlay.
