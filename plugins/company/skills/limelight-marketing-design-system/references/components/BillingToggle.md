# BillingToggle

The Monthly / Yearly switch above the pricing cards.

- The consumer provides `value` or `defaultValue`, `onChange`, and optional `save` text ("Save 20%"). The selected option has `aria-pressed`.
- Style: a `surface-300` track with a 2px inset and a 7px radius. The selected option is `surface-100` with `shadow-xs`. Labels are Geist 12px.
- Center it above the pricing grid, `space-10` above the cards.
