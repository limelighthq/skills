The closing call to action above the footer: one centered `heading-xl` line and one primary button ("Deploy your first AI employee now." · "Get started today").

- The consumer provides `title`, optional supporting text (children) and `action` (a primary `Button`).

- Use one per page, directly above `Footer`. Pricing uses a boxed version on `gradient-limelight` with faint orbit lines ("Start your 7-day free trial today").
