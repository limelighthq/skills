# CheckList

A short row of check-marked benefits under a resource title: "✓ How to identify top creators  ✓ How to track ROI".

- The consumer provides `items` (2–3 short phrases).
- Style: the site's 18px check glyph in `text-500`, 12px before each item. Items are `body-l` `text-500`, 24px apart, and wrap on small screens.
- Use it only for what a download contains. Do not use it for pricing features (use the `PricingCard` list) or for long lists.
