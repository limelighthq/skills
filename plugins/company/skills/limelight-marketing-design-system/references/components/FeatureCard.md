# FeatureCard

A brand icon, a small title and one or two sentences. The site uses it for "Who this is for", "The problem" and "Our values".

- The consumer provides `icon` (one of the Icons assets), `title`, optional `lead` (ink words) and the body (children).
- Default: a dashed `border-dashed` left rule, 24/20 padding, 50px between the icon and the text. Put 3–4 in a row.
- `boxed`: a 0.5px `border-card` box on `surface-100`, 32px padding and a 360px minimum height. Put boxes edge to edge in a row (their shared borders make one grid).
- Title: `heading-xs` (Host Grotesk 20px). Body: 14px `text-500`, with the lead in `text-600`.
