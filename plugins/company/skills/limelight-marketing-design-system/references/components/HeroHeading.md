# HeroHeading

The page headline: a muted lead-in, then the key phrase inside a dashed pill with a small peach dot ("Run creator, advocacy, and social programs `without adding headcount.`").

- The consumer provides `lead` and `highlight`. Put a `SectionTag` above it, a `body-l` line in `text-500` under it, and then the `AgentButton` + primary `Button` pair.
- Type: `heading-display` (Host Grotesk 52/62, -4.5% tracking, 32px on phones), centered. The lead is `text-400`, the highlight is `text-600` in a 0.4px dashed `surface-400` pill with a `glow-peach` dot.
- Use one per page, on `gradient-limelight` or the page's agent gradient. Keep the highlight to 2–4 words.
