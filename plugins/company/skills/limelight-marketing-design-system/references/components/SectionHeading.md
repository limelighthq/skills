# SectionHeading

A section's eyebrow plus a two-tone title: an ink phrase (`lead`, `text-600`) and a muted phrase (`rest`, `text-400`) on the next line. It can also have a short description on the right.

- The consumer provides `tag`, `lead`, `rest`, optional `description`, and `align` (`left` by default, `center` for stat and roster sections).
- Title: Host Grotesk 40/48 (`heading-xl`, 28px on phones). The site tracks it at -0.02em.
- The two tones carry the message: put the claim in ink and the qualifier in grey ("Four AI employees, / One connected system."). Do not bold anything.
- `text-400` is safe here because the title is 24px or larger on every ground.
