# Iconography

- **Brand icons** (Icons group): the site's feature icons. Each one is a Phosphor-style line glyph in `icon-blue` (#336791), standing on a small glass pedestal (stacked grey ellipses with a pale blue top). Use them at 42×44 at the top of a `FeatureCard`. Use the assets as they are: do not recolor the glyph or remove the pedestal.
- **UI icons:** the double carets (`icon-caret-double-left/right`) for carousels. For other small UI glyphs (dropdown caret, close, search), use **Phosphor Icons (Regular)** at 12–16px in `text-600`, which matches the Figma file.
- Do not use emoji or filled multi-color icons.
