# Icons

The live site's feature icons: a Phosphor-style glyph in `icon-blue` #336791 on a glass pedestal. They are full color, so do not recolor them. Size: 42×44 in a `FeatureCard`.

- `icon-robot`, `icon-heart`, `icon-share-network`: "Who this is for" (Ivy, Cooper, Elliot).
- `icon-target`, `icon-chart-line-up`: signals and attribution.
- `icon-apps`, `icon-workflow-path`, `icon-disconnected-plug`, `icon-trend-up`, `icon-bell`: "The problem" cards.
- `icon-rocket`, `icon-target-arrow`, `icon-users`, `icon-chat-dots`, `icon-repeat`: "Our values" (About page).
- `icon-path-curve`: agent execution steps.
- `icon-caret-double-left`, `icon-caret-double-right`: carousel arrows for `IconButton` (#336791 with a pale blue fill).
