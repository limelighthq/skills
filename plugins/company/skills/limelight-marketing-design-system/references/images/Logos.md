# Logos

Vector logos exported from the Figma handoff file ("Logo Full" and "Icon" frames). Ink: `limelight-black` #131313 or `limelight-white` #ffffff.

- `limelight-logo-black.svg`: full lockup (symbol + wordmark), 754×134. Use on white, off-white and `gradient-limelight`.
- `limelight-logo-white.svg`: the same lockup in white. Use only on `limelight-black`.
- `limelight-symbol-black.svg`: symbol alone (brackets + dot), 195×173. Use for favicon, avatar, slide footer and small spaces.
- `limelight-symbol-white.svg`: symbol in white, on `limelight-black`.

Keep clear space of at least the dot's diameter on every side. Never recolor, stretch, outline or put the logo on an agent gradient.
