# Logo

- Use the full lockup (`limelight-logo-black`) for primary brand placement. Use the symbol alone (`limelight-symbol-black`) for small spaces, favicons and slide footers.
- There are three approved grounds (guide 1.3–1.4): white/off-white and `gradient-limelight` take the black logo (`limelight-black`), and `limelight-black` takes the white logo.
- Do not recolor the logo, put it on agent gradients, stretch it, or rebuild the symbol. The symbol is two corner brackets framing a dot, like a camera viewfinder putting someone "in the limelight".
