# Overview

Limelight hires out AI employees for social: Ivy, Cooper, Lana and Elliot. The brand idea is **calm futurism**: soft gradients, diffused glows and airy layouts. The technology should feel human, ambient and frictionless, not "techy". Think a designed future, not a sci-fi future. AI agents should feel approachable and easy to work with.

Brand values: **Minimal · Modern · Clean · Trust · Approachable · Enterprise grade.**
