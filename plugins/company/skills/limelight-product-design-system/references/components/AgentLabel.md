# AgentLabel

The glass "{ Ivy }" pill under the agent portrait at the top of the panel.

## Consumer provides

- `name`: the agent's first name. The component adds the braces.

## Anatomy

- 14px/21 weight 400, `-0.04em`, `ink`; padding 3px 17px; radius `radius-label` (20px); `glass-fill` (white at 50%) with a 1px `glass-border`.

## Do / Don't

- Do use it only in the panel signature. Running text says "Ivy", never "{ Ivy }".
