# Avatar

The round initials avatar for a creator or teammate.

## Consumer provides

- `initials` (two letters), `variant` 1, 2 or 3 (D cycles three tints by row: row 1 = 1, row 2 = 2, row 3 = 3, then repeat), `size` `row` (26px, default) or `card` (32px), and `label` when the avatar stands alone.

## Anatomy

- Row: 26px, 9px initials at weight 400. Card: 32px, 11px initials at weight 450 (nav, Inbox). Round.
- Tints: `avatar-1-fill` / `avatar-1-ink`, `avatar-2-fill` / `avatar-2-ink`, `avatar-3-fill` / `avatar-3-ink`.

## D exceptions

- D's initials inks #82766D, #6B8374 and #7A7589 are 3.6 to 3.8:1 on their fills → darkened to 4.5:1 (`avatar-N-ink`).
