# ScoreChip

The green relevancy score chip in creator rows and cards ("94").

## Consumer provides

- `value`, and optional `max` and `label` for the accessible name (default "Relevancy 94").

## Anatomy

- 12px/16.2 weight 500, tabular figures, padding 4px 7px, radius `radius-chip` (5px), `positive-fill` ground, `score-text` (#297553) figure: 5.16:1.

## Do / Don't

- Do keep the chip green for every score, as in D.
- Don't colour-code the score. The number carries the meaning.
