# Content fundamentals

- **Agents are teammates.** Use first names: "Build with Ivy", "Chat with Elliot", "Ask your team anything…". The panel shows the name as "{ Ivy }" only in `AgentLabel`.
- **Case follows D.** Title Case on page titles, primary actions and some headers: "Run Discovery Now", "Your Wallet", "Sent By", "Add funds". CAPS on eyebrows, section labels and table headers: WORKSPACE, TEAM OPERATIONS, STATUS, CREATOR, IVY'S TAKE, QUICK ACTIONS. Status words keep the case the data carries: "Running", "Draft", "NEW". Everything else is sentence case as D writes it: "Internal targeting", "New campaign", "Creator outreach".
- **Counts are plain numbers with a noun:** "20 of 20", "20 need review", "0 selected for outreach", "42 creators in pipeline", "6 need your decision". Set figures in tabular numerals (`number`, `stat-m`, `stat-l`).
- **Insight cards report like a teammate:** a one-sentence finding as the heading ("25 of the 42 on Q2 SaaS Growth have never been contacted."), then one or two sentences of reason, then stats, tags and up to three actions. No hype, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- **Dates:** day + short month in rows ("Added 14 Sept"). Activity timestamps add the time: "Ivy · Example · 06:57 PM".
- **Dialogs** carry a title and a one-line subtitle under it (D prints "Limelight · Local prototype").
- **Empty and error states use D's strings:** "No matching conversations" with "Show all conversations", "No chats match.", "This link is unavailable".
