# Agent-marks

Copied from the Limelight marketing system: each agent's decorative element (guide 4.2), exported from the Figma "Decorative Element" slide as full-color SVG (240×240, glow + line art). D does not use these marks anywhere in the app. Keep them for future onboarding and empty-state art, each with its own agent. Do not recolor them: they are not single-ink.

- `ivy-mark.svg`: Ivy: crossing ellipses, pink core, blue glow.
- `cooper-mark.svg`: Cooper: meridian lines on aqua.
- `elliot-mark.svg`: Elliot: nested arcs to a point of light, periwinkle.
- `lana-mark.svg`: Lana: mirrored arcs (hourglass) on pink/peach.
