# Agent-references

The two reference images the live Proposal D prototype draws its agent art from, copied byte for byte from D (`/brand/ivy-reference.png`, `/brand/elliot-reference.png`). They are screenshots of the marketing agent pages, not portraits: D crops them with CSS.

- `ivy-reference.png` (1690×958): Ivy's portrait. `AgentFace agent="ivy"` crops it at background-size 563.333%, position 49.64% 82.07%.
- `elliot-reference.png` (1872×1260): Elliot's portrait (background-size 645.5%, position 46% 78.14%) and the shared agent-panel ground. The panel body shows it at `auto 200%`, anchored left bottom, under a 7% white veil (`gradient-panel-veil`) on `surface-panel`, for both agents.

Never show these images uncropped in the app.
