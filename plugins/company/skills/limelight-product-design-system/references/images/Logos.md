# Logos

Copied from the Limelight marketing system. In the app, `limelight-symbol-white` sits in the rail's workspace button on `primary-workspace` (#211F25), 36px, radius 10; D draws the same brackets-and-dot mark at 20px. The full lockup is for sign-in and empty-workspace screens (D shows neither). Vector logos exported from the Figma handoff file ("Logo Full" and "Icon" frames). Ink: #131313 (black files) or #FFFFFF (white files).

- `limelight-logo-black.svg`: full lockup (symbol + wordmark), 754×134. Use on white and light surfaces.
- `limelight-logo-white.svg`: the same lockup in white. Use only on a dark ground such as `primary`.
- `limelight-symbol-black.svg`: symbol alone (brackets + dot), 195×173. Use for favicon, avatar and small spaces.
- `limelight-symbol-white.svg`: symbol in white, on `primary-workspace` in the rail.

Keep clear space of at least the dot's diameter on every side. Never recolor, stretch or outline the logo.
